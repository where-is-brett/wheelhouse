Show the director the Logbook: read `wheelhouse/board.md` in the project and give it as it is, with nothing added. If it isn't there or can't be read, say only that the Logbook couldn't be read.

Lean's playbook (`wheelhouse-lean`) keeps the Logbook up to date; its section "The Logbook" says how and when. Its parts include "Ideas" and "Rulings", the ideas kept for later and the rulings the director confirmed, with "Nothing" when there are none.
