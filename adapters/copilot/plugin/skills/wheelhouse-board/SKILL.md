Show the director the board: read `wheelhouse/board.md` in the project and give it as it is, with nothing added. If it isn't there or can't be read, say only that the board couldn't be read.

Lean's playbook (`wheelhouse-lean`) keeps the board up to date; its section "The board" says how and when. Its parts include "Ideas" and "Rulings", the ideas kept for later and the rulings the director confirmed, with "Nothing" when there are none.
